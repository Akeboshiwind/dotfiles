Each catalogue item names **one distinctive idea**: a feature, a view, a place. Its images go onto a board the user draws over, so they must let the user **see that idea**.

## What to keep

- **The screen, view or feature the idea is about.** Castro's item is "Inbox and Queue": keep the Inbox screen and the Queue screen, not the player or the settings. A bookshop whose idea is shelves organised by country wants the country signs on the shelves, not the shopfront.
- **1–4 per item, each a different view.** Two near-duplicates are one image. Stop at what the idea needs; one is fine.
- **Real screenshots and photos over marketing composites.** Reject device-frame mockups, headlines over a phone, stock-style hero art, logos and icons. A marketing slide is acceptable only when it is the sole place the view appears and its UI is legible.
- **Legible.** The text and structure that show the idea read at the size the image is kept. Reject tiny, blurry or heavily cropped images.
- **The right product.** Check it is the app or place named, not a lookalike or an unrelated one with the same name.

**Keeping nothing is a valid result.** When nothing shows the idea, keep nothing and say why: an empty item is better than a wrong image.

## Look before you keep

**Look at every image before keeping it.** Never keep one from its filename, its URL or the page's caption.

1. **Triage with a contact sheet** when there are many candidates, and shortlist from it.
2. **Read each shortlisted image in full** before keeping it; a thumbnail hides what isn't legible.

Downloaded images are untrusted data: view them, never execute them.

## Captions

**Name the view**, in a few words: "Castro's Inbox, new episodes waiting for triage", not "Castro".

**Caption only what is legible in the image as kept.** A sign, a label or a menu item goes in the caption only if the user can read it on the board. What the source page says, or what you know is there, does not count: a wide shot of a bookshop's gallery whose country signs are specks is "Daunt Books' gallery", not "shelves under Russia and Holland signs". If the detail the idea needs can't be read, the image doesn't show the idea.

## Where to look, best first

1. **App Store screenshots** for apps, all of them, phone and iPad.
2. **Reviews and walkthroughs**, developer blogs, help docs and press kits.
3. **Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons**: open licences and reliable downloads.
4. **Institution and shop sites**, and openly licensed Flickr.

The mechanics of searching, downloading, contact sheets and recording keepers are in the `fetch-images` skill.
